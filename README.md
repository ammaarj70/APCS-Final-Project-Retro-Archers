Retro Archers is a 2D game where one player and bots standing on platforms try to knock each other off the platforms and into the abyss to win the game by shooting arrows at the enemy. If you shoot an enemy multiple times, they will lose all their health which is another way to win against them if they haven’t been knocked off. Clear all six waves to win the game. Every two waves, the number of enemies increases by one. There are multiple types of arrows with special abilities which will help the player win against the waves of enemies, who will be equipped with normal arrows. See controls for some more info.

These enemies, which are bots, will shoot arrows periodically and come in waves. Upon defeating an enemy, another will shortly spawn, and the more you defeat, the more that spawn in until you win the game. To prevent spamming arrows, players will have a stamina bar that regenerates slowly which will make it difficult to shoot arrows at a very fast rate in order to win easily. Enemies will start off shooting at you with little accuracy, but the longer they stay undefeated, the better their accuracy gets.

Apples will appear randomly which the players can shoot to gain health and/or stamina. A red apple will heal the player, a blue one will refill the player’s stamina bar, and a yellow apple will regenerate the player’s health and stamina. Nothing happens if a bot shoots an apple by accident.

Physics is implemented. The player will be able to use their stamina to jump up in the event that an arrow pushes them down. If the player loses all their health, they lose. If the player loses all their stamina, they must wait to regain some before shooting or jumping. 

Controls:
- Move the mouse to aim and hold down the mouse to shoot with more strength. The longer you aim, the more stamina you lose at a linear rate but your arrow will go faster. Release the mouse to shoot.
- Press the spacebar to jump. Unless you have already been knocked off, it will make you jump high and slightly forward. This allows you to remain on the platform if you’re closed to being knocked off or just dodge arrows. It takes up a significant amount of stamina.
- You can press the 1 (normal), 2 (stun: takes more stamina), and 3 (multishot: takes more stamina) keys to switch between different kinds of arrows, each with their own abilities. 
- Press R to restart the game if you lose (or win!)
